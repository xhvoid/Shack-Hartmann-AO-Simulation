Erratum: the 'atmosphere' block of five version-2 manifests

medium_tier_v2, medium_tier_v2_reused, medium_gate_tier_v2, full_tier_v2 and
reference_tier_v2 were written by a run_tier that read the atmosphere's travel
record after the one-frame component-timing probe, which resets the atmosphere.
Their 'max_travel_m' therefore reads 0.0 and 'periodic_band_repeated' false.
Nothing else in those manifests depends on it (the probe runs after the loop,
the science result and the solver statistics). run_tier now captures the record
before the probe; the regenerated ci_tier_v2 manifest has the correct value and
is otherwise identical (telemetry, science, configuration, system and calibration
hashes) to the run before the fix. The same five manifests also carry the old
capability-gate note ('20 frames do not establish settled AO performance'),
which is wrong for the 2000-frame reference run; the note now states what the
gate checks.

The travel is deterministic: fastest layer speed x (n_steps - 1) / frame rate.
