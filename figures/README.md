## Historical and regression figures

Other root-level figures document earlier exploratory/archived notebooks,
including high-order SCAO, the historical noise/latency proxy and experimental
PWFS studies. They are retained as research history and are not the source of
the current README's quantitative claims. In particular, historical high-order
plots use the older RMS-normalized atmosphere and same-frame correction.

`detector_level_SCAO/` contains accepted example and regression artifacts.
The packaged fast error budget uses a disturbance within the DM's controllable
space and contains no fitting error. Public-data-conditioned examples use
public inputs alongside explicitly synthetic AO assumptions; they are not
measurements of instrument performance. Consult
[validation](../docs/validation.md) and [provenance](../docs/provenance.md)
before comparing those figures with current physical-atmosphere studies.
